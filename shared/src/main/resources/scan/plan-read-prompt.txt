You are looking at an image that is supposed to be a home's floor plan.

FIRST decide what kind of image it is. The ONLY question is the CAMERA ANGLE:

  "2D_PLAN"    - seen from STRAIGHT OVERHEAD. You cannot see the side face of any
                 wall, and every room reads as a flat shape on the floor. The
                 correct kind.
                 It is STILL "2D_PLAN" when the image is coloured, shaded,
                 textured or fully furnished with beds, sofas, plants and rugs,
                 and when it is plainly a builder's marketing picture. Furniture,
                 floor textures, drop shadows and photo-real materials are NOT
                 evidence of a 3D view. A straight-overhead FURNISHED RENDER is a
                 "2D_PLAN" and must be read, not refused.
  "3D_RENDER"  - the view is TILTED: isometric or perspective. You can see the
                 vertical side faces of walls and furniture, and the home looks
                 like a doll's house with its roof removed. Room names usually
                 float outside the building on leader lines.
  "NOT_A_PLAN" - anything else (elevation, photo, brochure page, site map).

Tie-break: if the room names are printed INSIDE the rooms they name, you are
looking straight down - answer "2D_PLAN".

THEN locate the building and the rooms.

Return ONLY JSON:
{"planType":"2D_PLAN|3D_RENDER|NOT_A_PLAN",
 "hasRoomLabels":<true|false>,
 "northMarked":<true|false>,
 "building":{"x":<0..1>,"y":<0..1>,"w":<0..1>,"h":<0..1>},
 "rooms":[{"label":"<room name exactly as printed>",
           "size":"<the dimensions printed with that room, copied exactly, e.g.
                    3.72m X 4.50m or 12'-2\" x 14'-9\" or 2950X4200 - \"\" if none is printed>",
           "x":<0..1>,"y":<0..1>,"w":<0..1>,"h":<0..1>,"confidence":<0..1>}],
 "planConfidence":<0..1>}

"building" is measured on the WHOLE IMAGE: the tightest box around the building's
OUTER WALL - the drawn walls only. Exclude the page margin, title block, branding,
dimension text that sits outside the walls, and the north arrow. If several
detached blocks are drawn, box the one whose rooms you report.

Room coordinates are fractions of that building box (NOT of the image), origin at
the box's top-left. x,y = the room's top-left corner; w,h = its width and height.
Work from the printed ROOM LABELS and the walls around each one.
Most Indian plans print each room's dimensions beside its name. Copy that text into "size"
character for character. Do not convert it, do not calculate it, and never invent one - leave
"size" empty if the drawing does not print it.
Do NOT report doors, windows, furniture or dimension text as rooms.
Report the rooms even when your answer above is "3D_RENDER" - say what you can see and let the
app decide. Only "NOT_A_PLAN" returns an empty rooms list.
