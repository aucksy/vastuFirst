Place your rooms

Tap the grid to add a room. Tap a room to move or resize it.

Floor-plan editor

Interactive prototype at a real 360 dp phone width — the narrow, common case. One grid cell is 39 dp, smaller than the 48 dp minimum touch target, which is the constraint that shapes the whole design.

Try this

  • Pick a room type, then tap the grid — a 2×2 room appears.
  • Drag the room anywhere to move it — it snaps to the grid as you go.
  • Tap it to select — four round corner handles appear.
  • Drag a handle to resize. The size badge follows above your finger, never under it.
  • Tap empty space to deselect. Undo reverses any move, resize, add or remove.

Why these choices

  • Handles are 16 px drawn, 44 px touchable — a small target you can actually hit.
  • Handles sit on the corners, half outside the shape, so a 1-cell room can still be resized without the handles burying it.
  • The size badge sits above the shape, out from under the hand.
  • Drag moves relative to where you grabbed, so the room doesn't jump to centre under your finger.
  • The +/− buttons stay. Dragging is impossible with a screen reader and hard for shaky hands — every gesture keeps a button equivalent.

This is a prototype for agreeing the interaction, not the shipping build. Colours and type are the real Sage & Gold tokens.
