Guided-grid harness

This is the real floor-plan builder from the app, running here so you can feel it with your finger/mouse before it ships. It uses the identical placement, drag, resize, plot-size and door maths as the phone.

  • Pick a room, then press-and-slide on the plan — lift to place.
  • Touch a room and slide to move it; pull a corner dot to resize.
  • A room's middle always moves, at any size (even 1×1).
  • Rooms can't overlap — the preview turns red and the move is refused.
  • Plot size changes the shape; rooms re-pack to fit, never overlap, never dropped.
  • The front door snaps to the nearest outer wall of your rooms.
