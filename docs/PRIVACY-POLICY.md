# VastuFirst — Privacy Policy

**Last updated 14 October 2026.**

> These are the same words the app shows under **Settings → Privacy**. They are kept in one place in
> the code (`PrivacyScreen.kt`) so the published page and the app can never say different things.

---

## Your homes stay on your phone — except a plan picture you ask us to read

Everything you draw, every score and every report is worked out on this device and saved only on this
device. There is no account, no sign-up and no phone number.

## What we collect

Nothing about you. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location, and the app has no
account to sign in to. The homes you draw, their scores and their reports are stored only in the
app's own private storage on your phone, where no other app can read them.

## The one thing that leaves your phone

If you choose to have a floor plan read for you, that one picture is sent to be turned into rooms,
and the rooms come back. It goes to a relay called OpenRouter, which passes it to an AI model from
OpenAI — and sometimes to a second model from Google, for a second opinion. Their computers are
abroad. The app asks you before it does this, every first time, and you can turn it off again in
Settings. The picture is not stored by us and is not linked to you — there is nothing to link it to.
Drawing your home by hand needs no internet at all and is scored by exactly the same rules.

## Rule updates

Once a day, when your phone is online, the app asks our own server whether a newer set of Vastu
rules has been published, and takes it if one has. The request carries nothing about you or your
homes. Like any visit to a website, it shows our server your connection's internet address; we do
not use it to identify anyone.

## When the app crashes

If the app closes unexpectedly, it writes what went wrong into its own private storage and offers to
email it to us the next time you open it. Nothing is sent unless you press send, and you can read the
whole message first. It contains only technical detail, your phone model and the app version — never
your homes, your scores or anything about you.

## If you buy the full report

Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card, and the app does not
receive your payment details at any point. Google tells us only that this device has bought the
report.

## Deleting everything

Settings → Delete all my data removes every saved home from this device immediately and permanently.
Uninstalling the app removes everything too: the app does not let your phone's own backup copy your
homes or plan pictures to an online account or to a new phone. Because nothing is stored on our
side, there is nothing left for us to delete and nothing you need to ask us for.

## Children

VastuFirst is not aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone, of any age.
