## Full description (max 4,000 characters)

VastuFirst reads the layout of your home the way a Vastu consultant would — room by room — and tells
you plainly what works, what does not, and where each rule comes from.

**Add your home in the way that suits you**
• Upload a floor plan (a picture or a PDF) and we read the rooms straight off it
• Photograph a printed plan with your camera
• Or draw it on a simple grid — it never leaves your phone
You can correct any room before your score.

**Mark North, then get your score**
Turn the dial so North points the right way on your own plan. VastuFirst places every room in its
direction and scores the home out of ten.

**A report that says where to start**
• How your rooms read: already right, not ideal, need fixing
• Where to start, and why that one first
• Your front door, on one of 32 named positions
• Remedies that suit a flat as well as a house — tell it your home is a flat and it stops suggesting
  changes to the building around you, while still showing you what it found

**Honest about the tradition**
Every rule is tagged: traceable to a classical text, traditional practice, modern practice, or a
point where the schools disagree. Where the texts contradict each other, you see both readings side
by side, and which one your score follows. No fear, no urgency, no promises of wealth, health or
fortune — Vastu is guidance for your own decisions, not a guaranteed outcome.

**Private by design**
• No account, no sign-up, no phone number
• Your homes, scores and reports are saved only on your phone
• The only thing that ever leaves your phone is a plan picture you choose to have read — and the app
  asks first

**Free to try**
Your score and your first findings are free. The full report is one payment — no subscription,
nothing renews.
