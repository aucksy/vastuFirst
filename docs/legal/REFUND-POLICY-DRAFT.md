## The short version

The full report is a one-time payment. If something went wrong, write to **contact@vastufirst.com**
and we will put it right.

## On Android (Google Play)

- **Within 48 hours of buying,** you can ask Google Play for a refund yourself: open
  play.google.com → your account → Order history, or follow Google's "Request a refund" page. Google
  decides these requests under its own policy.
- **After 48 hours,** write to us at contact@vastufirst.com with the order number from your Google
  Play receipt (it starts with "GPA."). [[We refund within N days of purchase / We refund when the
  report could not be produced or was wrong because of a fault in the app.]]

## On iPhone (App Store) — when the iPhone version is released

Apple handles every refund for purchases made in the App Store. Ask at **reportaproblem.apple.com**.
We cannot issue App Store refunds ourselves.

## When we always refund

- You were charged twice for the same report.
- You paid and the report did not unlock, and restoring the purchase (on the unlock screen) did not
  fix it.
- The app could not produce the report you paid for because of a fault on our side.

## When we may not refund

Disagreeing with what the Vastu tradition says about a home is not, on its own, a fault in the app:
the report says plainly where its rules come from and where the tradition disagrees with itself.
[[Client's choice: refund anyway as goodwill within N days, or not.]]

## After a refund

The full report locks again on that store account. Your saved homes and their free scores stay.
