## What VastuFirst is

VastuFirst scores the layout of a home against a published set of Vastu Shastra rules and explains
the result: which rules it checked, where each rule comes from, and where the tradition disagrees with
itself. You draw your home on a grid, or ask the app to read a floor plan you photograph or upload.

## What VastuFirst is not

- **Vastu is a traditional practice. The app gives guidance for your own decisions — not a guaranteed
  outcome.** It makes no promise about health, wealth, relationships, luck or any other result.
- It is **not** architectural, structural, engineering, legal, financial or medical advice. Do not
  change a building, buy or sell a property, or make a financial decision on its score alone. Ask a
  qualified professional for anything structural or legal.
- The score is only as good as the plan it is given. A plan read from a picture can be misread; you can
  correct any room before your score, and you should check the rooms before relying on it.
- Where traditional texts disagree, the app shows both readings and says which one the score uses. That
  choice is ours, made openly; it is not a statement that the other reading is wrong.
